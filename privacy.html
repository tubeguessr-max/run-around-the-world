Privacy Policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

In short: there's no account and no sign-in. Your runs and progress stay on your iPhone. We don't run servers that store your data, we don't track you, we don't show ads and we never sell anything about you.

This policy explains how Run Around the World (“the app”), made by Kayem Studios (“we”, “us”), handles information. Kayem Studios is responsible for your information under UK data protection law (UK GDPR and the Data Protection Act 2018).

What stays on your iPhone

The app keeps everything it needs on your device: the runs, walks and hikes that count towards your journey (date, time, distance, duration and type), runs you add by hand, the route you've chosen, your settings and the milestones you've reached. This is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. We can't see it. It's removed when you delete the app, and it may be included in your own iPhone or iCloud backups, which Apple handles.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads from Apple Health only what it needs to move you along your route: your workouts (their type, start time, duration and distance) and walking and running distance, used to find the distance of a workout. It counts runs, walks and hikes, and never your daily step totals.

Health data never leaves your iPhone. The app doesn't write anything to Apple Health, and Health data is never used for advertising or marketing, shared with anyone, or stored in iCloud by the app. You can change or remove access at any time in the iPhone's Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Run Around the World.

Purchases

The Equator route is free. Other routes are unlocked with a one-time in-app purchase, made through Apple. Apple handles payment, and we never see your card or Apple Account details.

To check whether you've unlocked the routes, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that verifies App Store purchases. When the app starts or you buy or restore a purchase, RevenueCat receives a random identifier created for the app (not your name or email), your purchase receipt from Apple, and basic technical details such as your app version, iOS version, country or region and IP address. RevenueCat uses this only to provide the service to us. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

Photos

If you choose to save a milestone postcard to your photo library, the app asks to add that one image. It doesn't read your photos.

Other apps

The app doesn't connect to Strava, Garmin or other running apps. If those apps save your workouts to Apple Health, the app reads them from Apple Health as described above.

What we don't do

We don't use analytics, advertising or tracking tools, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your information.

How long we keep information

Information on your iPhone stays until you delete the app. RevenueCat keeps purchase records for as long as needed to provide its service and meet legal obligations. If you email us, we keep the conversation only as long as needed to help you.

Your rights

Under UK data protection law you can ask to access, correct or delete information about you, or object to how it's used. As your journey data stays on your device, you can delete it yourself by deleting the app. For anything held by RevenueCat or by us, contact us and we'll help. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Children

The app isn't directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update it here and change the date at the top. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.

Contact
