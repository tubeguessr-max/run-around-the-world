Getting your runs in

How do my runs count?

Choose running, cycling or both, then connect Apple Health in the app. Runs, walks, hikes and bike rides saved to Apple Health as workouts move you along your route, from the date your journey started. Daily step totals don't count, only workouts with a distance.

I record with Strava, Garmin or another app

Turn on that app's Apple Health sync so it saves your workouts to Apple Health. For example, in Strava go to Settings → Applications, Services and Devices → Health, and turn on sending activities. Usually only activities recorded after you switch this on are sent.

A run hasn't appeared

Check it's in the Health app as a workout with a distance. Then check the app can read it: in the iPhone's Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices → Globe Runner, turn on Workouts and Walking + Running Distance. Then open the app and tap Sync at the bottom of Today. You can also add a run by hand.

Routes

Can I change route?

Yes, at any time from the route name at the top of Today. Every km you've run counts on every route, so you start where your distance puts you.

What does “Unlock all routes” include?

The Equator is free. One purchase unlocks every other route, including routes added later. You can preview a locked route before buying. It's a one-time purchase, not a subscription.

I've got a new phone

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore purchases, signed in with the same Apple Account. Your runs come back from Apple Health once you connect it again (runs added by hand stay on the old phone, unless you restore it from a backup).

How do I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Your data

Your runs and progress stay on your iPhone, and there's no account. Deleting the app deletes them. See the Privacy Policy for details.
